Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Start here for a quick overview. For current eligibility and campaign terms, use the official app and help pages.

What is Funngro?

Funngro connects young people with brand campaigns and opportunities to build practical skills through real-world activities.

What kind of opportunities can I find?

Campaigns may include brand promotion, content creation, referrals, sampling, surveys, app testing and other activities. Availability depends on current campaigns and eligibility.

How do payments work?

Payment terms depend on the campaign. Review the task requirements, approval process and payout conditions in the Funngro app before participating.

Can brands use Funngro?

Yes. Brands can explore promotion, sampling, referrals, creator content, surveys, app testing, product ideation and other youth-focused campaign formats.

Where can I learn about trust and safety?

Visit the Trust & Safety page for information about verification, campaign review, payouts, data handling and support.
